Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our classes, fees, and enrollment.

How do I enroll in a class? ▼

Simply contact us via phone, email, or WhatsApp with your preferred grade and subject. We'll arrange a free consultation to assess your level, discuss your goals, and find the best batch timing for you.

What are the current fees? ▼

Fees vary based on grade, subject, and learning mode (home, group, or online). Please contact us directly for exact pricing. We offer flexible payment options and discounts for bulk enrollments.

Do you provide notes and study materials? ▼

Yes! Comprehensive chapter-wise notes, practice sheets, mock tests, and revision materials are provided with all classes. These are available both in printed and digital formats.

Can I switch from one batch to another? ▼

Yes, batch transfers are usually possible depending on availability. Please discuss with us if you need to change your timing after enrollment. We're flexible and want to accommodate your needs.

Do you offer online classes? ▼

Yes! We conduct live online classes via Zoom or Google Meet with the same quality and interaction as in-person sessions. You get all materials, recordings, and support just like home/group tution.

How often are classes held? ▼

Classes are typically held 2-3 times per week for 2 hours each session. The frequency can be adjusted based on your needs and exam schedule. Peak season (pre-board exams) may have more frequent sessions.

Is there a free trial class? ▼

We offer a free consultation and assessment session. You can discuss your needs, ask questions, and understand our teaching approach before making any commitment.

What is your class size? ▼

We keep group classes small (maximum 5-6 students) to ensure personal attention and quality interaction. Home tution is one-on-one, and online classes are also small-batch for better engagement.
