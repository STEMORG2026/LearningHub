## 1. Primary & Lower Secondary (Grades 1 – 8)

### Core Objective
Build a rock-solid foundation in mathematical intuition, scientific curiosity, and technical literacy to transition students seamlessly into high school STEM courses.

### Subject Breakdown
- **Mathematics**: Arithmetic, basic algebra, geometry fundamentals, spatial visualization, and word-problem translation.
- **General Science**: Introduction to physical sciences, biological organisms, chemical changes, environmental systems, and laboratory observation skills.
- **Computer Literacy**: Fundamentals of algorithms, binary systems, internet safety, and basic coding concepts.
- **English & Nepali**: Technical vocabulary, essay structuring, comprehension, and scientific writing skills.

## 2. SEE Board (Secondary Education Examination — Grades 9 & 10)

### Curriculum Standard
Fully aligned with the National Curriculum Development Centre (CDC) of Nepal for SEE board examinations.

### Key Courses & Modules

#### ⚛️ Physics & Chemistry (Compulsory Science)
- **Mechanics & Motion**: Force, pressure, energy, work, power, gravitational acceleration.
- **Heat & Thermodynamics**: Thermal expansion, heat capacity, state changes.
- **Light & Optics**: Reflection, refraction, lenses, human eye defects and corrections.
- **Electricity & Magnetism**: Ohm's Law, electromagnetic induction, household circuits.
- **Chemistry**: Periodic table trends, chemical bonding, reactions, metals/non-metals, organic compounds (hydrocarbons), industrial gases ($CO_2, NH_3$).

#### 📐 Optional Mathematics (Opt. Math)
- **Functions & Polynomials**: Composite functions, inverse functions, synthetic division.
- **Matrices & Determinants**: Cramer's rule, matrix inversion, linear transformation.
- **Coordinate Geometry**: Straight lines, pair of lines, circle equations.
- **Trigonometry**: Compound angles, multiple/sub-multiple angles, trigonometric equations.
- **Vectors & Transformation**: Vector addition, dot product, geometric transformations.

#### 🧮 Compulsory Mathematics & English
- Set theory, compound interest, arithmetic & geometric sequences, Mensuration, Statistics, and formal English grammar & composition.

## 3. NEB Board (National Examinations Board — Grades 11 & 12)

### Curriculum Alignment
Targeted for Grade 11 & 12 Science stream under NEB, serving as a dual preparation pathway for board examinations and Engineering (IOE) / Medical (IOM) entrance exams.

### Detailed Course Modules

#### ⚡ Physics (NEB 11 & 12)
- **Mechanics**: Vectors, projectile motion, Newton's laws, circular motion, rotational dynamics, gravitation, elasticity, fluid dynamics.
- **Thermodynamics**: First and second laws of thermodynamics, kinetic theory of gases, heat engines, entropy.
- **Waves & Optics**: Wave motion, interference, diffraction, polarization, optical instruments.
- **Electricity & Magnetism**: Electrostatics, Gauss's law, DC circuits, Biot-Savart law, Faraday's law, AC circuits.
- **Modern Physics**: Photoelectric effect, Bohr's atomic model, nuclear physics, semiconductor electronics, quantum concepts.

#### 🧪 Chemistry (NEB 11 & 12)
- **Physical Chemistry**: Stoichiometry, atomic structure, chemical equilibrium, ionic equilibrium, electrochemistry, chemical kinetics.
- **Inorganic Chemistry**: Periodic classification, s & p block elements, transition metals, metallurgy, coordination chemistry.
- **Organic Chemistry**: Haloalkanes, alcohols, phenols, ethers, aldehydes, ketones, carboxylic acids, amines, reaction mechanisms.

#### 📐 Mathematics (NEB 11 & 12)
- **Calculus**: Limits, continuity, derivatives, applications of derivatives, integration, differential equations.
- **Algebra & Trigonometry**: Complex numbers, quadratic equations, permutations & combinations, binomial theorem, matrices & determinants.

## 4. Cambridge GCE A-Levels (AS & A2)

### Curriculum Alignment
Targeted for Cambridge International AS & A Level Physics (9702), Chemistry (9701), and Mathematics (9709) examinations.

### Detailed Course Modules
- **A-Level Physics**: Physical quantities, kinematics, dynamics, forces, work/energy, momentum, electric fields, capacitance, magnetic fields, particle physics, medical physics.
- **A-Level Chemistry**: Physical chemistry, equilibria, reaction kinetics, organic synthesis, analytical techniques (NMR, IR, Mass spec).
- **A-Level Mathematics**: Pure Mathematics 1 & 3, Mechanics (M1), and Probability & Statistics (S1).
